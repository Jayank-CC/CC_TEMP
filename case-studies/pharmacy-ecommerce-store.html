Ecommerce Website For Healthcare Products

The client wanted to develop an online platform which will cater to all Client health needs. Shombhob is a provider of healthcare products, medicines, toiletries, baby care products, and pet care products. The portal allows customers to search and get the right product, for medicines that requires prescriptions, the solution asks users to upload image / pdf of doctor prescription.

Shombhob.com aims to be the premier pharmacy provider within Bangladesh. Considering the target demographic, the solution was developed as both a Web Portal as well as Mobile Apps for iPhone and Android devices. The solution is also integrated with POS so that orders placed via client’s brick and mortar stores can also be synced with the order stores.

User Experience Design & Backend Integration

The design of the portal and app was tailored to provide a simple, uncongested layout for users who may be looking medicines. The color palette selection was done to provide an Airy, Modern, Welcoming feel to the users visiting the website. The solution is integrated with Client’s POS, multiple Payment Gateways, SMS Provider in Bangladesh. The mobile app for Shombhob.com was developed using Flutter platform.
