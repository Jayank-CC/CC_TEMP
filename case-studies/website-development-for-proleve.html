Designed & Developed A Seamless Shopify Store For Proleve’s Premium CBD Products

Proleve, a top-tier wellness brand offering premium CBD products, needed to update its online face and make online sales easier for customers. The brand needed a scalable e-commerce solution that would accommodate customer growth, deliver an amazing customer experience, and included Backend enhancements. To this end, Proleve chose to work with Cloud Converge to produce a fully custom Shopify website – with the company’s focus to deliver intuitive navigation, improve site performance, and create a better shopping experience for customers!

Challenge & Solution

Proleve’s previous e-commerce approach was facing problems in scalability, performance, and user experience engagement. The online shopping experience needed to be easy, mobile-friendly, and conversion-based, with emphasis on customer experience leading to the decision to migrate to Shopify. Cloud Converge took an opportunistic approach to plan so that the transition and implementation would go effectively.

Cloud Converge outlined the web development process for the Shopify site and worked very closely with the client to understand what the client needed and how to deliver a solution that met their needs.

Developing a High-Performance Shopify Website: The primary aim of the project was to build a Shopify e-commerce site that represented Proleve’s brand and provided a seamless shopping experience. Our development team designed and build a custom Shopify theme with an easy-to-use yet visually attractive online interface to meet the needs of the Proleve target audience.

Optimized Product Catalog & Navigation: The web site was set up with optimized product catalog and obvious navigation with ease of browsing to improve product visibility as well as help buyers browse to products they were looking for while adding to the overall user journey experience. We developed the categories and filters to help our customers find and browse effortlessly.

Results & Impact

The launched Shopify website greatly enhanced Proleve’s e-commerce platform. The new user-friendly shopping environment increased customer interaction, boosted conversions, and improved sales. Cloud Converge helped Proleve streamline operations and offer a better online experience through automation and improving backend management.
