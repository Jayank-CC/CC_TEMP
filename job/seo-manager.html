Job Summary

We are seeking an experienced and results-driven SEO Manager to lead our organic search strategy and drive sustainable growth across diverse client accounts globally. The ideal candidate will have a deep understanding of Technical SEO, On-Page SEO, Off-Page SEO, content optimization, and analytics. You will be responsible for managing SEO projects and teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and implement successful SEO strategies to improve organic search rankings, drive traffic, and conversions.
  • Conduct in-depth keyword research and competitor analysis to identify growth opportunities.
  • Perform comprehensive technical site audits to uncover issues (crawl errors, site speed, architecture) and recommend fixes.
  • Lead the execution of advanced technical SEO initiatives (structured data, schema markup, Core Web Vitals improvements, mobile-friendliness, pagination, and canonicalization implementation).
  • Plan and execute Off-Page SEO campaigns, including link acquisition and authority-building strategies.
  • Monitor, analyze, and report on SEO performance using tools like Google Analytics, Search Console, and third-party tools.
  • Collaborate with content, development, and marketing teams to ensure SEO best practices are integrated into all digital initiatives.
  • Track, measure, and report SEO KPIs, ranking, traffic trends, and campaign outcomes.
  • Stay updated with search engine algorithm changes and industry trends, and proactively recommend improvements.
  • Manage SEO budgets, forecasts, and ROI tracking for various campaigns.
  • Provide mentorship to junior SEO team members and support their professional development.

Required Skills & Qualifications

  • 5+ years of hands-on experience in SEO, including Technical, On-Page, and Off-Page SEO.
  • Strong understanding of search engine algorithms, ranking factors, and best practices.
  • Proven expertise with SEO tools such as Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, Ahrefs, SEMrush, Screaming Frog, Moz, and Google Keyword Planner.
  • Deep knowledge of technical SEO concepts including rendering, indexing, crawlability, site speed, schema markup, and structured data.
  • Experience in optimizing websites for international and multi-lingual SEO.
  • Track record of successfully improving organic traffic, keyword rankings, and lead generation.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a data-driven mindset.
  • Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal, and presentation capabilities.
  • Experience working with CMS platforms such as WordPress or similar systems.
  • Familiarity with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and their implications for search engine optimization.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in managing SEO for large-scale websites or e-commerce platforms, dealing with enterprise-level architecture.
  • Knowledge of local SEO, international SEO, and app store optimization (ASO).
  • Experience with reporting and dashboard tools such as Looker Studio.
  • Familiarity with other digital marketing channels (PPC, Social Media) and how they interact with SEO.
