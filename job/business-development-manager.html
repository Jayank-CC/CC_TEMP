Job Summary

We are looking for a dynamic and result-oriented Business Development Manager with hands-on experience in Upwork bidding and client acquisition. The ideal candidate must have a proven track record of successfully securing IT projects on various bidding platforms and the ability to generate business opportunities for IT services.

Key Responsibilities

  • Generate leads and business opportunities through Upwork and other online bidding platforms.
  • Handle end-to-end sales cycle, from bidding, responding to client queries, negotiation, to project closure.
  • Identify, qualify, and secure new business contracts with international clients (US, UK, AUS, etc.).
  • Understand client requirements and provide customized IT solutions.
  • Prepare business proposals, pitches, and presentations.
  • Follow up with prospective clients and convert leads into business.
  • Maintain client relationships and ensure long-term business retention.
  • Collaborate with the technical team to estimate project scope, timelines, and cost.

Required Skills

  • 3+ years of experience in IT sales, bidding, and business development.
  • Strong understanding of web, mobile, and digital marketing technologies.
  • Proven track record of achieving sales targets.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Good knowledge of proposal writing and client handling.
  • Working experience with freelancing platforms like Upwork, Freelancer, Guru, Fiverr, LinkedIn, etc.
  • Strong negotiation and convincing skills.
  • Self-motivated and target-driven approach.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, IT, or related field.
  • Experience in handling international clients and international sales campaigns.

Key Requirements

  • Proven ability to drive the sales process from plan to close.
  • Strong business sense and industry expertise.
  • Excellent mentoring, coaching, and people management skills.
