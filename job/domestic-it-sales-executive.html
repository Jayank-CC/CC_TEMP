Job Summary

We are looking for a dynamic and result-oriented Domestic IT Sales Executive with 2+ years of experience in IT sales. The candidate will be responsible for generating leads, building client relationships, understanding client requirements, and driving sales for IT services and solutions within the domestic market.

Key Responsibilities

  • Generate leads and build relationships through cold calling, email campaigns, networking, and online platforms.
  • Identify client requirements and suggest suitable IT solutions/services.
  • Build and maintain strong client relationships.
  • Conduct sales presentations and product/service demonstrations.
  • Coordinate with technical teams to understand project scope and client requirements.
  • Prepare proposals, quotations, and contracts.
  • Negotiate pricing and close deals effectively.
  • Follow up, qualify, and report all sales targets.
  • Maintain accurate records of client interactions and sales activities in CRM tools.
