End-to-End AWS Consulting Services

Most AWS environments look stable until you look closer. You’ll usually find things like oversized instances, services added without much structure, or pieces that don’t scale the way they were expected to. We find what’s causing friction: cost, performance, or reliability, and fix those first.

01

AWS Architecture Design & Review

We go through how your environment is structured across services like EC2, VPC, RDS, and Elastic load balancing, and check how those pieces are actually interacting. In many cases, things have just grown without a clear structure. We clean that up, make the setup easier to manage, and ensure it holds when traffic or usage increases. For new environments, we follow AWS Well-Architected principles from day one.

02

AWS Cost Optimization

Cloud costs rarely spike overnight. They grow quietly through unused resources, oversized instances, or missed pricing models. We look at how your services like EC2, S3, RDS, and Lambda are actually being used today, and clean that up. Sometimes it’s rightsizing, and sometimes it’s switching pricing models. The goal is simple: reduce waste without affecting how things run.

03

AWS Security & Compliance

Security issues sit in IAM policies, misconfigured access, or gaps between services that were never reviewed together. We handle access controls, encryption where needed, and visibility with CloudWatch and GuardDuty. When you have compliance requirements, like SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, or ISO 27001, we get things straightened out rather than fixed afterwards.

04

AWS Migration Consulting

Migrations don’t usually fail because of the moving data itself. It’s everything around it, like dependencies no one mapped, timelines that were too optimistic, or systems that behave differently once they’re live. We map things properly, plan migrations in phases, and validate each step before anything goes fully live. Whether it’s rehosting, replatforming, or reworking parts of the system, the approach depends on what makes sense for your setup.

05

DevOps & CI/CD on AWS

Manual deployments drag teams down and pile on risk as time goes by. We set up CI/CD pipelines with tools like AWS CodePipeline, CodeDeploy, and connect everything with GitHub Actions or Jenkins. Releases get quicker and smoother, and you’ve got way less hassle, making things fit seamlessly with your overall AWS cloud strategy.

06

Managed AWS Support

Our 5-Step Consulting Process

Every AWS environment comes with its own constraints, like existing architecture, team bandwidth, cost pressures, and timelines. Our structure stays consistent; how we apply it adapts to your setup.

Step 1

Discovery & Assessment

We analyze your AWS setup across services like EC2, VPC, RDS, and IAM, understand cost patterns and security configs, and talk to your team as well. That’s usually where the real issues come out.

Step 2

Strategy & Roadmap

Once we understand the current setup, the next step is deciding what to change, and more importantly, what not to. Focus stays on impact: immediate fixes first, then a clear 60–90 day plan your team can follow.

Step 3

Implementation Support

We work with your team to apply changes across infrastructure, automation, and configurations, either directly or in an advisory role. As changes are made, everything is documented to ensure continuity and reduce long-term dependency.

Step 4

Validation & Testing

All changes are validated before production rollout. We use staging environments to verify behavior; run performance and load tests where required, and for migrations, execute parallel runs to confirm stability before cutover.

Step 5

Handover & Ongoing Support

Clear documentation. Walkthroughs. Knowledge transfer. After that, it’s really up to you: you can take over and manage things yourself or keep working with us for ongoing AWS consulting support if you want.

For Startups

Start Clean. Stay Flexible.

Early decisions in AWS tend to stay longer than expected. If the foundation isn’t set up right, costs rise quickly, and fixing things later becomes more complicated than it should be.

  • Keep the architecture simple. Build what you actually need, using services like EC2, S3, or Lambda only where they fit.
  • Make costs visible from day one, so nothing catches you off guard
  • Implement basic CI/CD and monitoring early so releases don't become a bottleneck
  • Maximize AWS Activate credits in a way that actually supports your growth
  • Fixed-scope and on-demand models available; no long-term commitment required
